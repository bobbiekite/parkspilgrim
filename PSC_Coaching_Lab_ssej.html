Why It Looks Like a Science Lab

Think of S&S and EJ like Biology and Physics courses, and the Coaching Labs like the science lab sections that pair with them. Just like a science course without its lab is incomplete, an experiential course without its paired Coaching Lab misses the piece that makes the experience count as experiential. The lab is where the reflection, or the OPPS framework, gets applied, session by session, to whatever the student is actually going through.

Course Sequence S&S, the “Biology” Track EJ, the “Physics” Track
Course 1, required BACP 3500, Integrative Project BACP 3400 or PPSS 2100
Paired lab BACP 2000, Industry Project Coaching Lab (Career Compass) BACP 2010 or BACP 2110, Educational Journey Lab I or II
Course 2, optional BACP 3350 or LOS 3300, by major The other of BACP 3400 or PPSS 2100
Paired lab BACP 2100, Service Project Coaching Lab (Career Catalyst) The other Educational Journey Lab

Not a perfect parallel to an actual science lab, nobody is pipetting anything, but close enough that it clicks immediately for advisors, accreditation reviewers, and new team members. You already know what a lab is for. This is that, applied to values and meaning making instead of biology and physics.

The OPPS Framework for Educational Journeys

Developed and field tested across 300+ NPS sites. This is the operating system underneath the Educational Journey side of the Passport, and it is where values actually get named, tested, and shaped.

Stage Core Question What Happens Here
Orient Why am I going, and what am I hoping to find? Naming an intention before arrival, shifting from "where to go" to "why go." Research the site's nature, heritage, and civic layers so you arrive aware of all three.
Plan How much time do I need, and does this plan still feel right? Vetting the itinerary, whether alone or with others, and checking your overall feeling about the plan. Finalizing by making reservations.
Participate What is this place asking of me, right now? Being fully present at the site, hiking, reflecting, engaging staff, allowing for the unplanned (weather, fear, a chance encounter) to become part of the meaning.
Share What changed, and who else needs to hear it? Returning home and metabolizing the experience, journaling, storytelling, follow up action, so the experience continues to shape life after the visit.

Credit and Non Credit Options, Two Kinds of Delivery

PSC Degree Students

The educational guide is present for Orient and Plan (Session 1, aligned to the start of the paired course) and for Share (Session 2, near the end). The actual Participate phase, the pilgrimage itself, happens independently. Students choose an NPS site based on what is near them, what they are willing and able to travel to, and whether an in person or virtual experience fits their life. The guide is not designed to be there for that part. The student walks it alone, then brings what happened back to the guide to process.

OLLI & PWD Members

Like the Florissant Fossil Beds and Amache Educational Journeys, an educational guide (chaplain) is there for all four phases, together, as a group. There is no independent Participate phase. The group orients together virtually, then plans, participates, and shares together across a single full experience. A fundamentally different delivery model for a different audience, people who want the whole experience held for them, not people building a credential who need flexibility to complete a requirement around a job and a life.

Both are legitimate applications of OPPS as Educational Journeys. A PSC student who wants the fully guided version can join an OLLI EJ or participate through the Professional Workforce Development non credit offering of an EJ instead of the independent Participate model.
